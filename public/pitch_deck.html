⚠️ The Problem

$800B Informal Savings Market is Broken

Over 1 Billion people rely on informal ROSCAs (chit funds), but traditional operations face critical vulnerabilities:

🚨 Organizer Fraud

Unlicensed organizers run off with member contributions. Zero recourse or recovery mechanisms.

📜 Zero Credit History

Years of on-time ROSCA savings build 0 credit history for informal savers with traditional banks.

🔒 Rigid Payout Rules

Fixed lottery payout orders do not serve members needing urgent liquidity or working capital.

💡 The Solution

RotaFi: Trustless ROSCAs on Stellar

Soroban smart contracts replace informal organizers with automated, transparent code.

⚡ Soroban Smart Contract Engine

Automated cycle rotation, deterministic pot distribution, and on-chain audit trail.

Members bid discounts for early payouts; savings are refunded back as member dividend rebates.
